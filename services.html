Services

Storage, retrieval and digitization

Everything your records need, handled by one team and tracked in one portal.

See pricing

Physical storage

Standard cartons, legal-size boxes and oversized or media containers. Every container gets a barcode and a shelf location you can see in your portal.

  • Barcode scanned at every move
  • Retention dates tracked per box
  • Certified destruction when you approve it

Retrieval and delivery

Order a full box or a single file from your portal. Choose next-day delivery, same-day rush, or a visit to our reading room.

  • Track every request from pending to delivered
  • Sealed courier delivery, signed for by your contact
  • Return pickups scanned straight back into storage

Digitization

Document prep, scanning, indexing and OCR, including bound volumes. Receive files by secure download and choose to keep, return or shred the originals.

  • Searchable PDF, plain PDF or TIFF archive
  • Standard and rush turnaround
  • Files stored in your portal Documents page

How a retrieval works

Four steps from a click in your portal to the box in your hands, and back again.

  1. In your portal

    Request

    Pick a box and choose a delivery option.

  2. At our facility

    Pull

    Our team scans the box out of its shelf location.

  3. To your door

    Deliver

    Sealed courier delivery, signed for by your named contact.

  4. Back on the shelf

    Return

    Request a pickup and the box is scanned back into storage.

Next-day delivery is included. Rush same-day delivery is $18 extra.Request a retrieval
