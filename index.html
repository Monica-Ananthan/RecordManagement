Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about requests, scanning, security and cost.

Contact our team
How do I request a box?

Sign in to the client portal, choose the box and pick a delivery option. Next-day delivery is included, and rush same-day delivery is available for an extra fee.

Can you scan documents instead of delivering the box?

Yes. Request scanning for any box and the finished files appear in your Documents page. You choose whether the originals go back on the shelf or are destroyed.

How do I know my records are secure?

Every facility uses badge and PIN access, camera monitoring, climate control and fire suppression, and each box is barcode-scanned whenever it moves. See our security details.

What does it cost?

Storage, retrieval and scanning are priced separately, with no setup fee on accounts over 100 boxes. View pricing.

What happens when a retention period ends?

The box is listed in your portal for approval. Nothing is destroyed until you approve it, and you receive a certificate of destruction afterwards.
